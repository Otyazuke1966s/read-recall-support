What the app does

Open a PDF textbook or workbook and its printed red text is found automatically, ready to hide under a red sheet. You can also mark more parts to hide with the Trace, Lasso, Box and Table tools.

  • Sheet: slide the red sheet and recall the hidden answers.
  • Peel: reveal answers one at a time, like peeling sticky notes.
  • Q&A: after seeing the answer, rate yourself with four buttons, and the app decides when to ask again (spaced repetition).

The built-in user guide (English and Japanese) explains every feature.

FAQ

Does it cost anything?

The app is free to download, and reading PDFs is always free. The user guide and one book you add can use every feature for free. To mark and study more books, make a one-time in-app purchase (no subscription).

How is the free book chosen?

The first time you tap Study or Tool in a book you added, the app asks whether to make it your free book. Once chosen, it can’t be changed.

I got a new device or reinstalled the app. Do I pay again?

No, as long as you use the same Apple Account. Tap “Restore Purchase” on the purchase screen.

Can my family use it?

Yes. The purchase supports Family Sharing, so members of your Family Sharing group can use it at no extra cost.

How do I add books?

Add a folder from the Files app to the shelf, open a PDF from another app with Share, choose photos, or take photos of handouts and notes. Photos are turned into a PDF book.

Where are my marks and study records?

Only on your device. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them too. The app never changes your PDF files.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS / iPadOS 18.0 or later. The app is in English and Japanese; switch with the [E/J] button at the bottom left of the shelf.
