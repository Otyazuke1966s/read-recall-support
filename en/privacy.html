Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This page explains how the iPhone and iPad app “Read! Recall!” (the “App”) handles your information.

1. Information we collect

The App does not collect any information about you. There is no account, and no analytics, advertising or tracking. The App does not communicate with any server run by the developer.

2. Information stored on your device

Your marks, study records, settings and the book you chose as your free book are stored only on your device. They are never sent to the developer or any third party, and they are deleted when you delete the App (the free-book record is kept in your device’s Keychain so that it survives reinstalling).

The App never changes the PDF files you open.

3. Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you take photos of handouts or notes to make a book. Photos you choose are used only to make a book. The photos and the PDFs made from them stay on your device. The App asks for permission before using the camera for the first time.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple’s App Store. The developer does not receive your name or payment details. The App checks whether you have made the purchase with the App Store, on your device.

5. Children

The App does not collect information from anyone, of any age.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated along with the date above.
